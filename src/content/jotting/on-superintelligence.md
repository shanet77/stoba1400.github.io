Hearing about Jacob Coxon's Twitter/X thread[^1] on A.I. "superintelligence", and hearing once more (unfortunately) about the "permanent underclass", I needed to write something quickly. If this is a belief you or someone you know holds, I beg of you to broaden your horizons. This is not a problem that the world is actually facing. 
<br>
We see water and climate crises, violence, and wealth inequality. Many of these stem from A.I., but from the [production](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/06/1167658) [of](https://www.nahb.org/advocacy/industry-issues/land-use-101/tools-and-research/tools-pages/data-centers-outbidding-home-developers) [the](https://issueone.org/articles/reeling-from-major-lawsuit-losses-big-tech-injects-huge-sums-into-influence-operations/) [technology](https://cdt.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Full-Text-Algorithm-driven-Hiring-Tools-Innovative-Recruitment-or-Expedited-Disability-Discrimination.pdf), not the "intelligence" of the system. These concerns are born out of a wealthy[^2] bubble that cannot, or perhaps refuses to, understand the plights of others[^3]. These groups of people are convinced _their_ fears are greater than what a "normie" can perceive.
It is another example of the insular tech industry believing it knows better than the [world](https://www.businessinsider.com/bill-melinda-gates-foundation-education-initiative-failure-2018-6), as the [rationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rationalist_community) and [Effective altruist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_altruism) communities attempt to assert their worldview as objective.


[^1]: I'm not linking it here as I don't want to promote this more than it has been (and more than I technically have).
[^2]: This largely describes the disconnect between A.I. the tool and its direct [effects](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/17/nx-s1-5954414/residents-file-lawsuit-challenging-noise-pollution-from-elon-musks-data-center) in communities.
[^3]: Both domestically and internationally. China is not reaching "superintelligence" either, nor does its work justify pouring more resources into this at the cost of everyone and everything else.
